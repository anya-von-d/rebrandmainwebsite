Lending to Friends is Broken

The Problem

  • No written agreement means no accountability
  • Asking for repayment feels deeply uncomfortable
  • Rising cost of living is making informal lending more common, and riskier
  • No existing tool is built for lending — only splitting or payments

There is no formal product for the most common form of lending on earth: one person helping another person they care about. So people figure it out with text reminders and Venmo screenshots. And when it breaks down, it is not just money that is lost.

$184B

in informal loans between Americans each year — completely untracked

75%

of people who lend or borrow with friends say they are no longer as close afterward

37%

of Americans can’t cover a $400 emergency expense with cash or its equivalent

Competitive Landscape

Built for What No One Else Does

Informal IOUs

No tracking. No reminders. No legal standing. Disappears the moment it’s needed most.

Splitwise

Expense splitting only. No loan agreements, no repayment schedules, no payment processing.

Namma

Contracts, schedules & reminders — but no payment processing. Monthly subscription. Documentation without execution.

Vony

Contracts + schedules + reminders + integrated payments — all in one. No subscription. Pay only when money moves.

Our competitors optimise for payments or expense splitting. Vony is the only platform designed around the full lending workflow — contracts, repayment plans, interest, automated enforcement, and dispute documentation built in. We solve the relationship problem, not just the transaction.

Revenue

How We Make Money

Instant Transfers

~1%

Fee for instant bank withdrawal. Standard transfers are free, creating a natural upgrade path — the Venmo model.

Card Payments

2–3%

Processing margin on card-funded repayments. High-frequency, recurring revenue tied directly to loan volume.

Affiliate Revenue

Variable

Credit card referrals for users needing larger lines of credit. The NerdWallet model — high margin, zero overhead.

Revenue scales with loan volume, not headcount. Each new loan creates compounding transaction revenue, repeat engagement, and referral growth — a flywheel that accelerates as the user base grows.
