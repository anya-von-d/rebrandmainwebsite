What is Vony?

Vony is a micro-lending platform that makes it safe and simple to lend or borrow money with people you know.

Create a loan, set the terms, sign digitally, and let Vony handle the rest. And if borrowing from friends or family isn’t the right fit, Vony will connect users with other lending options to find the best path forward.

Our Mission

To make peer lending feel as easy as sending a text, while giving both sides the structure and protection they deserve.

Vony does the chasing

The Problem

Informal lending is one of the most common financial behaviors in the world, yet it remains almost entirely unstructured. People lend money through texts, Venmo payments, and verbal promises, with no shared system for accountability, repayment tracking, or clear expectations. As economic pressure rises, these loans are becoming larger, more frequent, and more emotionally complicated.

Modern fintech has reinvented nearly every part of consumer finance, payments, investing, banking, even credit, while largely ignoring one of the most common forms of lending that already exists between people.

Proven Demand

Our interviews showed the same pattern across all kinds of lending situations, from friends covering short-term expenses to families helping during financial emergencies. The lender wants to support someone they care about without having to chase them later. The borrower feels the loan sitting quietly behind every text and interaction.

Both sides want structure and clarity, but neither knows how to introduce it naturally. Vony was built to make that easier.

Everyday friend lending

Rent gaps, dinners, trips. Short-term, $200–$2K, high emotional stakes.

Emergency family loans

Medical bills, car repairs, moves. Planned, $2K–$20K, relationship-defining.

Revenue Model

Instant Transfers

~1%

Fee for instant bank withdrawal. Standard transfers are free, creating a natural upgrade path, the Venmo model.

Card Payments

2–3%

Processing margin on card-funded repayments. High-frequency, recurring revenue tied directly to loan volume.

Affiliate Revenue

Variable

Credit card and loan referrals for users needing larger lines of credit. The NerdWallet model, high margin, zero overhead.

Every loan pulls in at least two users and generates multiple repayments. Transaction revenue compounds with each repayment, each new borrower, and each referred lender, the business grows with usage, not with hiring.

Competitive Landscape

Informal IOUs

No tracking. No reminders. No legal standing. Disappears the moment it’s needed most.

Splitwise

Expense splitting only. No loan agreements, no repayment schedules, no payment processing.

Namma

Contracts, schedules & reminders, but no payment processing. Monthly subscription. Documentation without execution.

Vony

Contracts + schedules + reminders + integrated payments, all in one. No subscription. Pay only when money moves.

Vony’s advantage is focus. We are the only product built specifically around the hardest part of peer lending, enforcing repayment without destroying the relationship. Agreements, reminders, payment plans, and exportable records are native, not bolted on. And when a peer loan isn’t the right fit, we guide users to better options instead of pushing them into a bad deal. That positioning is hard to copy from a payments app or an expense tracker.
