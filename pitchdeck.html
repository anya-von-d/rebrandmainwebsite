What is Vony?

Vony is a micro-lending platform that makes it safe and simple to lend or borrow money with people you know. Create a loan, set the terms, sign digitally, and let Vony handle the rest.

That means: a digital agreement both sides sign, a repayment schedule with optional interest, automated reminders so no one has to ask, in-platform payment tracking, and exportable records if a dispute ever arises. Structure without the awkwardness.

Our Mission

To make peer lending feel as easy as sending a text — while giving both sides the structure and protection they deserve.

The Problem

  • No written agreement means no accountability
  • Asking for repayment feels deeply uncomfortable
  • Rising cost of living is driving record levels of informal lending, with no safety net when it goes wrong
  • No existing tool is built for lending — only splitting or payments

There is no formal product for the most common form of lending on earth. So people figure it out themselves — and when it breaks down, it is not just money that is lost.

Proven Demand

Across income levels, our user interviews surfaced the same pain in two forms: everyday lending between friends — rent gaps, trips, dinners — and emergency lending within families — medical bills, car repairs, moves. In every case, the emotional dynamic was identical. The lender wants to help and dreads having to ask. The borrower feels the weight at every dinner, every text. Both sides want structure. Neither knows how to ask for it. That is the need Vony was built to resolve.

Everyday friend lending

Rent gaps, dinners, trips. Short-term, $200–$2K, high emotional stakes.

Emergency family loans

Medical bills, car repairs, moves. Planned, $2K–$20K, relationship-defining.

Revenue Model

Instant Transfers

~1%

Fee for instant bank withdrawal. Standard transfers are free, creating a natural upgrade path — the Venmo model.

Card Payments

2–3%

Processing margin on card-funded repayments. High-frequency, recurring revenue tied directly to loan volume.

Affiliate Revenue

Variable

Credit card referrals for users needing larger lines of credit. The NerdWallet model — high margin, zero overhead.

Every loan pulls in at least two users and generates multiple repayments. Transaction revenue compounds with each repayment, each new borrower, and each referred lender — the business grows with usage, not with hiring.

Competitive Landscape

Informal IOUs

No tracking. No reminders. No legal standing. Disappears the moment it’s needed most.

Splitwise

Expense splitting only. No loan agreements, no repayment schedules, no payment processing.

Namma

Contracts, schedules & reminders — but no payment processing. Monthly subscription. Documentation without execution.

Vony

Contracts + schedules + reminders + integrated payments — all in one. No subscription. Pay only when money moves.

Vony’s advantage is focus. We are the only product built specifically around the hardest part of peer lending — enforcing repayment without destroying the relationship. Agreements, reminders, payment plans, and exportable records are native, not bolted on. And when a peer loan isn’t the right fit, we guide users to better options instead of pushing them into a bad deal. That positioning is hard to copy from a payments app or an expense tracker.
